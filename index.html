Instructions

  1. Click New Outer Bound to start an outer boundary.
  2. Click or tap the map to add points.
  3. Drag the points to adjust their positions.
  4. Click Finish Current Bound when you have at least 3 points.
  5. Click New Outer Bound again to create another outer boundary.
  6. Click New Inner Bound to create an inner boundary.
  7. You can create as many outer and inner boundaries as you need.
  8. Use Clear All Outer or Clear All Inner to remove one type.
